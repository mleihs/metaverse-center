The Loop

Sechs Systeme, ein Rhythmus. So sieht die erste Woche aus.

Day 0
The Forge

You Type One Sentence

“A drowned republic trading in tides and secrets.” Minutes later Saltmeridian exists: 28 citizens with opinions, 17 buildings, three rival guilds and a newspaper.

Day 1
Living Agents

You Sleep. They Don’t.

Your citizens form opinions, hold grudges, fall in and out of alliances — and print all of it in the morning broadsheet. You read what your world did overnight.

Day 2
Epochs

The Cycle Resolves at 18:00 UTC

You joined a competitive season. Your spy sits in a rival’s archive; their saboteur sits in yours. Orders lock, the cycle resolves, five scores move.

Day 3
Resonance Dungeons

Four Go Under. Three Come Back.

Zone stability is slipping, so you send a party into The Deluge. The water rises 5cm per room. One agent returns carrying something the flood kept for centuries.

Day 4
Drift

A Träger Crosses the In-Between

Between the worlds lies the Drift — a playable node-sea. Your traveler docks at a neighbor’s broadcast edge and hauls home foreign cargo through the static.

Day 5
The Substrate

Real Earth Interferes

An election happens — on the real one. It resonates through every simulation: your broadsheet runs riot, two zones destabilize. The multiverse noticed.

Then the next cycle begins — and the broadsheet writes about all of it.
